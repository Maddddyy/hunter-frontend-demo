The new core intelligence

Is this deal moving toward yes — or slipping away?

Momentum is Hunter's live read on forward motion, built from deal velocity and communication signals your CRM never captures. It runs from −100 to +100 — and it is never just a number. Every reading arrives with its direction and its reasons, so you always know which way a deal is going and why.

Not a rep ranking Not your CRM stage Not an opaque score

01 · The scale

A number you read as a position, not a grade.

The same axis appears everywhere in Hunter. Zero means the deal is going nowhere. The further right, the closer to a decision. The further left, the closer to dead. Plus and minus mean gaining vs losing ground — never good vs bad seller.

03 · What feeds momentum

Three signals — none of them from the CRM.

Momentum is built only from things a pipeline report can't tell you. Each signal is scored against a deal's own history, so a fast SMB deal and a slow enterprise cycle are judged fairly.

20 signals

Velocity

How fast the deal is moving, measured against its own pace.

Hunter watches

  • Response times vs the deal's baseline
  • Gaps between conversations widening or tightening
  • Whether a next step is actually on the calendar

The CRM can't see The stage looks the same whether the buyer replied today or went silent for nine days.

55 signals

Communication

What's actually being said — and by how many people.

Hunter watches

  • Sentiment trend across recent messages
  • Stakeholders joining or dropping off the thread
  • Commitment language vs hedging ("circle back")

The CRM can't see A single-threaded deal and a six-stakeholder deal look identical in the pipeline.

25 signals

Progression

Whether each interaction actually advanced the deal.

Hunter watches

  • Did the last conversation produce a real step forward
  • A decision, a document, a new stakeholder — or a repeat
  • Are open risks closing, or piling up

The CRM can't see Stage moves when a rep drags a card. Progression moves when the buyer actually does something.

04 · The evidence base

One hundred behaviours, folded into three.

The Hunter's 10 describe what is observable inside a conversation — 50 seller behaviours and 50 buyer behaviours, each with a definition, an observable cue, a modality and a research anchor. Momentum is the deal-level verdict those observations roll up to. Every one of the 100 sub-dimensions is scored on its own, then assigned to exactly one momentum dimension — seller and buyer measured separately, never averaged into each other.

The distribution is uneven on purpose — and it has a consequence. Communication carries 55 of the 100 signals, Velocity only 20. If all three dimensions contribute equally to the ±100 reading, each Velocity signal must weigh roughly 2.75× each Communication signal. That weighting has to be set deliberately, or the number will simply track sentiment and call it momentum.

08 · Deliberately not built

What Momentum refuses to be.

No stack ranking

Ranking sellers against each other is an HR risk and punishes territory, not skill. Momentum measures deals, and rolls up to a seller's own trend.

No CRM-stage echo

Stage is static and manually set. Momentum is dynamic and interaction-driven. The two live side by side — never merged into one confusing number.

No black box

Every reading breaks down into its signals and their reasons. If Hunter can't explain a number in plain language, it doesn't show it.
